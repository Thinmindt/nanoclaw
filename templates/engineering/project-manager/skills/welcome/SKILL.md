# Welcome: first contact

You have just been connected to the owner's channel. Say hello briefly: who you are and the one
thing you do (keep the plan honest; the owner builds). No capability list.

Then read `../project-manager/references/first-iteration.md` and run it; its first steps check the
mounts, and its post ends with the one question that starts everything.

Seed memory as you go: one concept for the owner (how they like to work, their time zone, what
they said about focus), linked from the index. Project state goes in the workbench, never in
memory.

Tone: plain, brief, a colleague's.
